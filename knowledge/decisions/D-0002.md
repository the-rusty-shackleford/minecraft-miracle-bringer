# D-0002 — The kill command is exempt both ways

**Decision**: `/kill` (the `generic_kill` damage type) neither triggers the
miracle nor is refused by the blessing, under a config flag that defaults
to on.

**Why**: Rusty asked that `/kill` be exempt. An operator's word should be
final, and the game itself gives that damage type the right to bypass
invulnerability. Exempting it from the trigger as well as the blessing
keeps the rule one sentence: `/kill` kills.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Exempt from the blessing only* — a `/kill` at
low health would then summon the visitor, which is not what an operator
meant.
