# D-0004 — The visitor is a mindless mob moved by position, never saved

**Decision**: The visitor is a `Mob` with no AI and no gravity, invulnerable,
unpushable, unclickable, never saved, drawn with the player model in the
mod's skin and a halo layer. He hovers and rises by setting his position
each tick.

**Why**: A living entity is what the player model and the humanoid
renderers expect, and a mob with no AI is the cheapest one: no goals, no
pathing. Never saving him means a world reloaded mid-visit has no visitor
rather than one standing for ever. He is moved by position because the
gametest for his ascent found that a mob with no AI never runs the step
that applies velocity.

**Alternatives rejected**: *A plain `Entity`* — no humanoid renderer fits
it. *An armour stand with a head* — no skin, no halo.
